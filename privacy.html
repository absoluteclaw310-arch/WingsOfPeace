Privacy Policy

Information Collection

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. This typically happens when you use our contact form to inquire about our services or book a dove release. The information collected may include your name, email address, phone number, and event details.

How We Use Your Data

Any information you provide is used solely for the purpose of coordinating your event and providing the requested services. This includes:

  • Communicating with you regarding booking and event details.
  • Coordinating delivery and release timing.
  • Answering questions via our contact form.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is used only for the administration of our services and is not shared outside of our immediate operational needs except when required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of the data we have on file for you or to ask us to remove your information from our records. To do so, please contact us through the contact form on our home page.
